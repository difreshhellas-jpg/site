Everything about Travel Mouthwash

Formula Strength

Advanced antiseptic with 0.12% chlorhexidine gluconate. Kills 99.9% of oral bacteria within 30 seconds.

Duration of Protection

12-hour freshness and bacterial reduction. 24-hour plaque reduction with twice-daily use.

Usage

15ML per rinse (~15–20 seconds). One bottle = 8–10 rinses depending on usage.

Bottle Capacity

50ML total. Lightweight, leak-proof screw-cap. TSA-compliant for air travel.

Ingredients

Alcohol-free, fluoride-enriched, xylitol for cavity prevention. No artificial dyes or harmful additives.

Flavor Profile

Spearmint, eucalyptus, and wintergreen blend. Fresh, non-overpowering, clean finish.

Gum Health

Helps reduce gum inflammation and bleeding. Ideal when regular brushing is missed. Suitable for sensitive gums.

Shelf Life

36 months unopened. Once opened, use within 6 months for maximum efficacy.

Clinical Testing

Dermatologist and periodontist tested. Safe for daily use. Non-toxic in small accidental amounts.
