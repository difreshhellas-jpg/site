Everything about Tauro Delay Spray

Active Ingredients

Vitamin E (antioxidant), Aloe Vera (soothing), and a lidocaine derivative (mild topical anesthetic). Hypoallergenic formulation.

Application Method

Spray onto desired area 5–10 minutes before activity. ~2–3 cm coverage per spray; 2–3 sprays typically sufficient.

Effectiveness Duration

Results typically last 30–45 minutes of active performance. Reapplication possible if needed.

Bottle Size

18ML with precision spray nozzle for controlled application. Approximately 80–100 applications per bottle.

Safety Profile

Non-irritating, dermatologically tested, safe for sensitive skin. Does not affect condom integrity or partner comfort.

Storage

Cool, dry place (15–25°C). Avoid direct sunlight. Keep away from children. Shelf life: 24 months.

Quick Absorption

Fast-drying, absorbs within 3–5 minutes. Leaves no residue or discoloration on skin or clothing.

Ingredient Transparency
