Product Details

Everything about the Perfume Atomizer

Refillable Design

Twist-top screw mechanism for easy refilling. Compatible with standard 5ML bottles and roll-ons. Reusable across 50+ refill cycles.

Spray Technology

Fine mist nozzle for consistent, even coverage. Single spray = 0.1ML (~50 sprays per atomizer).

Capacity & Duration

5ML lasts approximately 20–30 days of daily use (2–3 sprays per application).

Material Durability

Anodized aluminum body resists corrosion and tarnishing. Impact-resistant coating. Sealed against leakage during travel.

Travel Safety

TSA-compliant 5ML size. Does not violate airline liquid restrictions. Secure screw-cap prevents accidental discharge.

Fragrance Compatibility

Works with eau de toilette, eau de parfum, cologne, and essential oils. Maintains fragrance integrity without degradation.

Maintenance

Wipe exterior with a soft cloth. Clean nozzle monthly with warm water. Replace mist head if clogged.

Color Specifications

Black (matte finish, professional), Golden (luxury aesthetic, premium appeal), Silver (minimalist, timeless).
